◉ Animated lesson · Step 3.3 of 5 Slide 4 / 7

Constructing the distance-amplitude correction curve from three reference reflectors.

Watch how reflector depth shifts the echo amplitude while gate position remains fixed. The DAC curve is a calibration reference — not a limit — and it compensates for attenuation with increasing path length.

FIG 4 · DAC CURVE — SDH @ 12 / 25 / 50 mm ×1.25
◉ Key takeaways
Note · 01
DAC compensates for attenuation — a 50 mm reflector can be as significant as a 12 mm one once corrected.
Note · 02
Always calibrate with the same probe and couplant as the inspection. Re-verify after 4 h.
